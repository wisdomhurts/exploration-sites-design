Legal

Privacy Policy

How Exploration Sites collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us — including the confidential client material at the heart of our work.

Last updated June 28, 2026
01

Information we collect

When you reach out — through our enquiry form, by email, or by booking a discovery call — you give us details such as your name, company, role, email address, phone number, and a description of your project. If you book a call, our scheduling provider also records the time you choose and anything you submit with it.

We also receive limited technical information automatically when you visit the site: your browser and device type, approximate region, and the pages you view. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of majority.

02

How we use your information

We use what you share only to respond to your enquiry, to scope, quote, and deliver the work you engage us for, to send invoices and manage our working relationship, and — where you have asked us to — to keep you updated on your project.

We do not use your information for third-party advertising, and we never sell or rent it.

03

Confidentiality of client material

Most of our work involves non-public, commercially sensitive material — geological data, drill results, resource models, corporate presentations, and other information about exploration and mining companies, some of it material non-public information (MNPI) about publicly traded issuers.

We treat this material as strictly confidential. We access it only to perform the work, store it with access limited to the team members involved, and return or delete it on request. We do not trade in our clients’ securities on the basis of information we receive, and we do not disclose client material to anyone outside our team without authorization or a clear legal obligation. Where a client requires it, we sign a non-disclosure agreement before work begins.

04

Service providers & sharing

We rely on a small number of established services to run our business, and your information may be processed by them on our behalf: website hosting (Vercel), form submissions (Formspree), call scheduling, and standard email and cloud-storage tools. Each is bound to use the information only to provide its service to us.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only with these providers as needed to deliver our work, where we are legally compelled to, or in the event of a business transfer — in which case any successor would remain bound by this policy.

05

Cookies & analytics

We use minimal cookies and privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages are visited and how the site performs. This data is aggregated; it is not used to identify individual visitors or to track you across other websites. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings and the site will continue to work.

06

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and project information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected — to respond, deliver the work, meet our legal and tax obligations, and maintain our business records — after which we delete or anonymize it. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

07

Your privacy rights

If you are in Canada, you have the right under PIPEDA and British Columbia’s Personal Information Protection Act to access the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and withdraw consent.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you additionally have rights under the GDPR to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, email us using the details below; we will respond within the timeframe the law requires.

08

International visitors

Exploration Sites is based in Victoria, British Columbia, Canada, and our service providers may store and process information in Canada, the United States, and elsewhere. If you contact us from outside Canada, you consent to your information being transferred to and processed in these locations, which may have different data-protection laws than your own.

09

How we protect information

We protect your information with reasonable administrative and technical safeguards — limited team access, reputable service providers, and secure transmission. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10

Changes & how to reach us

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top reflects the current version, and material changes will be posted on this page.

Questions about this policy, or requests to access, correct, or delete your information, can be sent to info@explorationsites.com or by mail to Exploration Sites, Victoria, British Columbia, Canada.
