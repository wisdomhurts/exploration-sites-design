Privacy Policy

How Exploration Sites collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us.

01

What we collect

When you contact us through the website, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, company, email, and project details. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell your data.

02

How we use it

Submitted information is used only to respond to your inquiry, deliver contracted work, and send invoices. Project files and communications are stored on standard business software (email, cloud storage, CRM) with access limited to our team.

03

Analytics

We use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages are visited and how the site performs. This data is aggregated and does not identify individual visitors.

04

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access or delete your data, can be sent to info@explorationsites.com.
