Disclosures

Exploration Sites is a communications, design, and visualization studio. This page explains the limits of what our work and the figures on this site represent.

01

Not a dealer or adviser

Exploration Sites provides communications, design, and visualization services only. We are not a registered securities dealer, adviser, or investment fund manager in any jurisdiction. Nothing on this site is investment advice or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security, and we do not solicit investors on behalf of our clients. Investment decisions are yours, made with your own advisers.

02

Company figures are as reported by each issuer

Where this site references a client’s drill results, resource estimates, financings, corporate actions, or other technical or financial information, those figures are as reported by the company in its own public disclosure. They are presented for context, not as our representations. Mineral-project information is subject to NI 43-101 (or the equivalent in the company’s jurisdiction); for the complete, qualified-person-reviewed disclosure — including assay context, true widths, and resource categories — refer to each company’s filings on SEDAR+ and its own website.

03

The “combined market cap” figure

Any combined or aggregate market-capitalization figure shown is the sum of the most recently available market caps of the publicly listed companies referenced, drawn from third-party market data. It changes continuously with the market, is provided for context only, and does not represent value created by Exploration Sites. We do not move drill results or share prices.

04

Client names and logos

Company names and logos identify organizations Exploration Sites has produced work for. Their inclusion reflects work performed; it is not an endorsement of Exploration Sites by those companies, and it is not a recommendation of any security. All trademarks are the property of their respective owners and are used for identification only.

05

Results vary; no outcome is guaranteed

Case studies and examples describe work we delivered and context the companies themselves disclosed. They are not promises of results. Clear communication can help a market understand a story — it does not create geology, financings, or share-price performance, and past engagements are not indicative of future outcomes. We do not guarantee any capital-raising, market, or valuation result.

Links to company filings and third-party sites are provided for convenience; we are not responsible for their content. Questions about these disclosures can be sent to info@explorationsites.com.
