Why it matters

Where grade lives.

A plan map shows where you drilled. A section shows what you hit. For an investor weighing a discovery, the question that decides the valuation is the one a section answers: does the mineralisation continue at depth, and does it hold its grade?

We build drill plans, cross-sections, and long sections straight from your collar, survey, and assay tables — intercepts logged against lithology and structure, grade shown honestly, and a vertical scale that doesn’t flatter the body. Long sections project the whole deposit onto one plane, so an investor can see continuity, plunge, and open extensions in a single figure.
