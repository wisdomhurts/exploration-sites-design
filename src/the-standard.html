The Standard

The bar we hold our work to.

Institutional-grade isn’t a claim we make — it’s the standard we build to, and what we advise every client toward. This is the bar we hold our own work to: the same desk that scores your materials, turned on our own.

Our first commitment

The final call is always yours. We’ll make the case for this standard — hard, and every time — then build what you decide. Our commitment is to you before it’s to our own preferences. That’s the order, always.

  1. 01

    It survives a portfolio manager.

    Built to hold up under the most skeptical desk in the room — that’s what we build every deliverable toward.

  2. 02

    It carries our name only when it’s true.

    If we wouldn’t defend it in front of your board, we’ll tell you before it goes out.

  3. 03

    Every number is sourced.

    Grade, width, market cap, comparable — each one defensible to the drill log or the filing.

  4. 04

    No cherry-picked intervals.

    We show the assay in context, not the one number that flatters.

  5. 05

    Geology before design.

    We read strike, dip, and grade before we choose type and colour. The science leads; the craft serves it.

  6. 06

    A qualified person can stand behind it.

    Technical figures are built to survive QP and regulatory review, not just to look the part.

  7. 07

    Coordinates are verified, not assumed.

    Every pin is checked against tenure and datum before it’s placed on a map.

  8. 08

    Colour follows commodity, not decoration.

    Gold reads gold, copper reads copper — one consistent legend across every asset.

  9. 09

    Every map has a scale bar and a north arrow.

    Orientation and size are never left for the reader to guess.

  10. 10

    The basemap serves the story, not itself.

    Terrain and context are dialled back so the target reads first.

  11. 11

    Continuity is shown, not asserted.

    Mineralisation appears in section or 3D — not a scatter of plan-view dots.

  12. 12

    Section lines are drawn on the plan.

    The reader always knows exactly where the section was cut.

  13. 13

    One visual language across the program.

    Maps, sections, decks, and site read as one company, not five vendors stapled together.

  14. 14

    It reads on a phone.

    If it fails when skimmed between meetings, it fails. Legible small, every time.

  15. 15

    It survives the forward.

    The story holds when the deck lands in an inbox without you there to narrate it.

  16. 16

    It prints without falling apart.

    Line weights and type hold at letter size and at booth scale.

  17. 17

    The thesis fits on slide one.

    A non-geologist can say what you have, and why it matters, in a single sentence.

  18. 18

    A scale anchor frames the prize.

    Tonnes, ounces, or contained metal sit beside a comparable the market already knows.

  19. 19

    There’s a dated catalyst calendar.

    What happens next, and roughly when — a reason to keep watching after the deck closes.

  20. 20

    Capital structure is shown, not buried.

    Shares, options, warrants, and holders — no dilution surprises three slides in.

  21. 21

    Management is a record, not adjectives.

    Prior discoveries, builds, and exits stated factually — not the words “world-class.”

  22. 22

    Forward-looking statements are flagged.

    Disclaimers are built in from the start, not bolted on at the end.

  23. 23

    Nothing overstates the science.

    No adjective does the work a number should be doing.

  24. 24

    Pre-disclosure material is handled like the market-moving information it is.

    Discretion is the product. Your trust is the thing we protect first.

  25. 25

    Websites load fast.

    A heavy page loses the reader before the thesis ever lands.

  26. 26

    Every site is accessible.

    Keyboard-navigable, legible contrast, alt text on the figures — by default, not on request.

  27. 27

    Turnaround is a commitment, not a hope.

    When we quote 24–48 hours, that’s the bar — guaranteed, not aspirational.

  28. 28

    A second set of eyes reviews everything.

    No deliverable reaches you without an internal review against this list.

  29. 29

    The craft is invisible.

    Aligned rules, optical spacing, balanced line weights — the details you’ll never consciously notice.

  30. 30

    If it’s only pretty, it’s not done.

    Beauty is the byproduct of clarity, never the goal.

How we hold it. Every deliverable clears an internal review against this list before it reaches you. Where something falls short of the bar, we’ll say so plainly — then build what you’ve chosen. It’s the reason our clients hand us pre-disclosure material and stop checking the work.
