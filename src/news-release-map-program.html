The idea

Your maps, on a cadence — not a quote.

Most news-release maps are the same map with new drill data. So we build your base once — the house-styled property base, the drill-hole and label template, the legend — and lock it. After that, each release is a fast, faithful update, not a fresh project.

You get a set number of maps every month, produced to one standard, with a guaranteed 48-hour turnaround — from clean data and priority over one-off work. Predictable for your desk; consistent for your investors.

The terms

Written for the budget memo.

  • Term. A 4-month minimum — one drill season, four monthly payments — then month-to-month. Cancel any time after the fourth month.
  • Pause. Between programs, pause for up to six months with your locked base retained. Resume within twelve months and the setup fee is never charged again.
  • Capacity. Your tier buys standing capacity and priority, not map inventory — unused maps don’t carry forward.
  • Guarantee. Turnaround is measured from clean data, and it’s guaranteed.
  • Billing. One-time base setup of ${{ prices.nrmpSetup }}, billed with your first month. Maps beyond your tier at the ${{ prices.nrmpOverage }} member rate. Monthly, in CAD.
Who it’s for

Companies in an active program.

If you’re drilling and releasing results on a regular cadence, a map should never be the thing that holds a release. The Program is built for explorers and developers with a steady flow of news — so every result lands with a clear, consistent, investor-ready map, and your house style compounds instead of drifting release to release.

Confidentiality

Unreleased results stay unreleased.

The Program handles material information before it’s public, and we treat it that way. A standing NDA is available on request. Access is restricted to the team assigned to your account, and unreleased results are held off shared systems until your release crosses the wire.

Our staff do not trade in client securities. That’s a standing policy, not a case-by-case call.

Built for your QP

Your data. Your QP’s sign-off.

The Program fits inside your disclosure process, not around it. Your team sends the data; we draft the map on the locked base; your Qualified Person reviews and approves it; then it goes out with the release. We never publish anything — approval stays with you.
