Investor Hub · AI

An AI layer for the sector.

It surfaces the news, peers, and signals that matter to your story — and helps your IR team move faster on what they find.

What you get

A faster read on your sector.

It watches the news, benchmarks your peers, and flags shifts in sentiment, then helps with first drafts so your team spends less time gathering and more time deciding. You stay in control — it informs the work, it doesn’t replace your judgement.

It is built on the same large language models your team already knows, pointed at the exploration and mining sector and wired into the sources that matter to a junior’s IR — news feeds, filings, and peer disclosures.

How it works

Where it saves your team time.

News monitoring
Tracks coverage of your company, your commodity, and your district — and surfaces what changed, so nothing material slips past on a busy week.
Peer benchmarking
Pulls together what comparable companies are saying and doing — useful context before a release, a financing, or a board update.
Drafting assistance
First drafts of release language, FAQs, and investor replies, in your voice — a starting point your team edits, never an autopilot.
Sentiment signals
A read on how coverage and the conversation are trending, flagged early enough to respond rather than react.
