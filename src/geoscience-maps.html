Why it matters

The evidence behind the targets.

A locator says where; geoscience maps say why here. Lithology and structure, soil and rock geochemistry, magnetics, gravity, and IP chargeability are the layers that let an investor trace a target back to real, surveyed evidence — the difference between a story and a thesis.

We build each one as a designed figure: interpolation reviewed and approved by your technical team, the right colour ramp for the data, clean contours, and a legend a generalist can read. Often we stack several datasets — geology under geochem under geophysics — so the coincident anomaly that ranks the target reads in a single glance.
