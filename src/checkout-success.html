Before the kickoff

What to send us.

The kickoff is where we lock your base map. Having these ready means your first release map can start the same week. Send them to info@explorationsites.com, or bring them to the call.

  • Your GIS data: what the base map is built from. The first two matter most; send the rest if you have it.
    Claims & tenure
    Needed. Claim or licence boundaries, with claim numbers.
    Drilling to date
    Needed. Collars (hole ID, easting, northing, elevation), downhole surveys (azimuth and dip), and assays by interval, or just the drill-database export.
    Surface sampling
    Rock, soil, stream-sediment, or channel samples, with locations and results.
    Geology & geophysics
    Geological mapping, structures, and survey grids or interpretations (magnetics, IP, EM, gravity).
    Context
    Roads, infrastructure, neighbouring deposits, topography or LiDAR, and imagery.
    Existing figures
    Your last few news-release maps and deck slides (PDFs are fine). They show us what the market has already seen.
    Shapefile, GeoPackage, KML, CSV or Excel, DXF, and GeoTIFF all work, as do QGIS and ArcGIS projects. Tell us the coordinate system (for example, NAD83 UTM Zone 9N). With each new set of results after that, send the new holes’ collars, surveys, and assays. The 48-hour clock starts when that data is complete.
  • Your house style: logo files, brand colours, and a recent news release so we can match the look.
  • Your QP: name and email for the qualified person who signs off each map.
  • Your release calendar: results expected in the next few months, so we can plan capacity.
