[ §04 / 04 ]  Method

How an engagement works.

No open-ended estimates. No surprises. Approval before any work begins.

  1. 01/05

    Discovery call

    We learn about your deposit, your raise, and your timeline — and where the gaps are in how the market sees you.

  2. 02/05

    Scope & quote

    We define the deliverables and return a clear, fixed quote — no open-ended estimates, no surprises.

  3. 03/05

    You approve

    You review the scope and quote, ask questions, and give us the go-ahead before any work begins. Engagements are paid in full at approval — it’s how fixed quotes stay fixed.

  4. 04/05

    We build

    We produce the work to an institutional standard, with checkpoints so you stay informed throughout.

  5. 05/05

    Ongoing support

    As your story advances, we keep your materials current — on a project basis or under a retainer.
