Why it matters

The idea, before the data.

Most investors aren’t geologists. A conceptual figure does the work a wall of cross-sections can’t: it shows, in one clean diagram, what kind of deposit this is and why the company believes it’s here. Porphyry shell, epithermal vein system, orogenic gold at depth — the model is the thesis.

We build these as honest schematics, not artist’s impressions: a deposit model grounded in the real geology, a footprint comparison that sizes the opportunity against known analogues, a stratigraphic column that explains the host, or a site plan that shows how the project will be built. Accurate enough for the technical reader, clear enough for everyone else.
