How it works

From a stack of cards to a system.

Investor database
One clean record of every investor, broker, and analyst you’ve met — with the context of how and when, so nobody falls through the cracks after a show.
Segmentation
Contacts sorted by who they are and how engaged they are — existing holders, warm leads, sell-side — so the right message reaches the right list.
Email nurture
A steady cadence of updates that keeps your story in front of interested investors between releases — written to inform, not to spam.
Roadshow follow-up
A simple way to capture conversations on the floor and follow up while the meeting is still fresh — the step most juniors lose.
Reporting
A clear view of list growth and engagement — the kind of summary that reassures a board that IR effort is compounding.
