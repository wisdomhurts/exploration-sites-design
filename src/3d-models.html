Investor Maps

3D isometric models.

3D isometric and perspective views that let investors grasp the scale, shape, and setting of a deposit at a glance — terrain, drilling, and orebody in one frame.

Why it matters

Geology you can turn over.

Plans and sections each show one face of a deposit. A 3D view shows the whole thing at once — the orebody sitting in its terrain, the drill holes that define it, and the depth and orientation that flat figures flatten away. For many investors it is the moment the project finally clicks.

We build these from your DEM, drill database, and any wireframes or block models you have, rendered as a designed figure rather than a raw software screenshot — controlled lighting, a sensible vertical exaggeration, and labels that hold up in a deck or on a website. The result reads as real ground, not a graphic.

The property in its setting
