What to send us.

The audit goes faster when we have your current materials in front of us. Send them to info@explorationsites.com, or bring them to the call.

  • Your current materials: the latest corporate deck, fact sheet and the maps and figures you use most, as PDFs or source files.
  • Your website: the address, and admin access or your web contact if we’re taking over updates.
  • Your GIS data: claims, drilling and any sampling, geology or geophysics behind your maps. Shapefile, GeoPackage, KML, CSV or Excel, DXF and GeoTIFF all work; tell us the coordinate system.
  • Your calendar: expected results, conferences and any financing in the next two quarters, so we can plan your credits around them.
  • Your house style and QP: logo files and brand colours, and the name and email of the qualified person who signs off technical figures.
