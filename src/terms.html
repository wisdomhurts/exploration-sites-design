About these terms

“Exploration Sites”, “we”, and “us” mean Exploration Sites Corp., a company based in Victoria, British Columbia. “You” means the company or person we do the work for.

These terms apply to every engagement: online Map Program subscriptions, retainers, and project work. Each engagement is made up of these terms plus the written quote, proposal, or order you accept — accepting by email or by paying counts. If we both sign a separate agreement, it takes precedence where it differs from these terms.

02

Project work

Quotes. Each project is priced on a written quote that sets out the scope, deliverables, number of revision rounds, and timeline. Quotes are valid for thirty days.

Payment. Projects are paid in full when you approve the quote, and work starts once payment is received — it is how fixed quotes stay fixed.

Changes in scope. Work outside the quoted scope, or revision rounds beyond those included, is quoted separately before we do it. We won’t bill for extra work you haven’t approved.

Timelines. Timelines run from receipt of payment and the data and materials we need. If data, feedback, or approvals arrive late, the timeline moves by at least the same amount.

03

Map Program subscriptions

Agreement. When you complete checkout for a News-Release Map Program tier and accept these terms, you enter a subscription with Exploration Sites on these terms.

Billing. At checkout we charge the one-time base setup fee and your first month. Your subscription then renews monthly on the same date and is charged automatically to the payment method on file through our payment processor, Stripe.

Minimum term & cancellation. Subscriptions have a four-month minimum term — four paid months — then continue month-to-month. You can cancel at any time by emailing accounts@explorationsites.com. After the minimum term, cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period and nothing further is charged. If you cancel during the minimum term, the remaining months of the minimum term stay payable.

Pause. You may pause at any time for up to six months by emailing us. Billing stops while paused, your locked base is kept, and paused months do not count toward the minimum term. Billing restarts when you resume. If you have not resumed by the end of six months, your subscription ends, and any unpaid months of the minimum term become payable.

Rejoining. If you come back within twelve months of your last billed month, the setup fee is not charged again.

Capacity. Your tier buys a monthly number of maps and priority over one-off work. It is capacity, not inventory: unused maps do not carry forward. Maps beyond your tier are billed at the member rate on your next invoice. New base maps, cross-sections, 3D models, and area-play maps are quoted separately.

Turnaround. “48 hours” means two business days. If we receive clean, complete data by noon Pacific time on a business day, the map is delivered by noon Pacific two business days later; data received after noon counts from the next business day. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding British Columbia statutory holidays. The 48-hour turnaround covers up to two maps per request; for larger batches we agree a delivery schedule with you. “Clean, complete data” means everything the map needs — final assay or result values, collar and trace data, and any text you want on the map — in a usable digital format. The clock restarts if the data or results change. Your revision requests are turned around on the same basis.

Refunds. The setup fee is non-refundable once base-map work has begun. Monthly fees are non-refundable once a billing period has started, except where the law requires otherwise. If we end your subscription for any reason other than non-payment or breach of these terms, we refund the prepaid fees for the unused part of the billing period.

Failed payments. If a payment fails, Stripe retries it automatically. We may pause work while an invoice is more than fourteen days overdue, and may end the subscription if it is more than thirty days overdue.

Price changes. Prices do not change during your minimum term. After that, we give at least thirty days’ notice by email of any price change, so you can cancel before it applies.

04

Retainers

Term. Retainers have a twelve-month term, billed monthly in advance. After twelve months they continue month-to-month until either of us ends them with thirty days’ written notice (email is fine). If you end a retainer during the first twelve months, the remaining monthly fees of the term stay payable.

Capacity. Each tier includes a monthly amount of production capacity, described on our site and in your proposal. Up to 40% of a month’s unused capacity rolls into the following month only; it does not accumulate beyond that and has no cash value. Work beyond your capacity is quoted before we start it.

Response time. Priority response times are measured in business hours (Monday to Friday, Pacific time, excluding British Columbia statutory holidays) and mean we acknowledge and schedule your request within that time, not that the work is finished.

Price changes. Prices are fixed for the twelve-month term. After that, we give at least thirty days’ notice by email of any change.

Your data & approvals

Your data. You are responsible for the accuracy and completeness of the data, results, and text you give us, and you confirm you have the right to let us use it for the work. We build maps from what you supply; we do not independently verify assays, resource figures, or other technical data.

Your approval. Before you publish anything we make — in a news release, presentation, filing, or anywhere else — you are responsible for reviewing and approving it, including review by your qualified person where your disclosure requires one. Publishing a deliverable confirms you have approved it.

Errors. If you find an error that we introduced, tell us within thirty days of delivery and we will correct it at no charge, as quickly as we reasonably can. Corrections for errors in data you supplied, or changes after approval, are billed as new work or count toward your tier.

Confidentiality

Much of what you send us is non-public, and some of it may be material information about a publicly traded company. We keep everything you share confidential: we use it only to do your work, limit access to the team members involved, and never disclose it without your permission or a legal obligation to do so. No one at Exploration Sites trades in a client’s securities using information we receive from that client. We return or delete your materials on request, keeping only what we must for legal, tax, and accounting records. We will sign a non-disclosure agreement before work begins if you ask.

Ownership & portfolio

What you own. Once the work is paid for, you own the final deliverables — the finished maps, figures, presentations, and other files we deliver — and may use, reproduce, and adapt them for any purpose. For subscriptions and retainers, ownership of each deliverable passes when the invoice covering it is paid.

What we keep. We keep ownership of our working files, templates, house styles, symbol libraries, and methods, which we may reuse for other clients without your confidential content. Working files (such as GIS projects and layered design files) are not included unless your quote says so.

Third-party material. Some deliverables include third-party material such as base-map data, satellite imagery, government geoscience data, or licensed fonts and images. That material stays subject to its own licence and attribution terms, which we follow when we build your work.

Portfolio. We may show finished work in our portfolio and marketing, and name you as a client — but only after the work, and the information in it, has been made public by you. We never show unreleased results. If you would rather we did not show a piece of work, tell us and we will take it down.
