02

Map Program subscriptions

Agreement. When you complete checkout for a News-Release Map Program tier and accept these terms, you enter a subscription agreement with Exploration Sites on these terms. If we later sign a project agreement with you for the same program, that agreement takes precedence.

Billing. At checkout we charge the one-time base setup fee and your first month. Your subscription then renews monthly on the same date and is charged automatically to the payment method on file through our payment processor, Stripe. All amounts are in Canadian dollars; GST is added where it applies.

Term & cancellation. Subscriptions run for a four-month minimum term, then month-to-month. You can cancel at any time by emailing accounts@explorationsites.com. Cancellation after the minimum term takes effect at the end of the current billing period. If you cancel during the minimum term, the remaining months of the minimum term stay payable.

Pause. Between drill programs you may pause for up to six months by emailing us; billing stops while paused, your locked base is retained, and paused months do not count toward the minimum term. Resume within twelve months of your last billed month and the setup fee is not charged again.

Capacity. Your tier buys standing capacity and priority each month, not map inventory — unused maps do not carry forward. Maps beyond your tier are billed at the member rate on your next invoice.

Turnaround guarantee. Your tier’s turnaround is measured from our receipt of clean, complete data, Monday to Friday.

Refunds. The setup fee is non-refundable once base-map work has begun. Monthly fees are non-refundable for a billing period that has started, except where required by law. If we end your subscription for reasons other than non-payment, we refund any prepaid fees for the unused part of the billing period.

Failed payments & price changes. If a payment fails, Stripe will retry it; we may pause work while an invoice is more than fourteen days overdue. We will give at least thirty days’ notice of any price change, and prices do not change during your minimum term.
