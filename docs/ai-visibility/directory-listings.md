## Where to list, and what each needs

| # | Directory | Why it matters | What it takes | Category / field to pick |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Google Business Profile** | Local answers ("studio in Victoria/Vancouver", Q22) and reviews (ChatGPT's one doubt about you). Feeds Gemini and Google AI Overviews. | Free. Google verifies by phone, video or postcard. Choose **service-area business** so the home address stays hidden. | Primary: *Graphic designer*. Add: *Website designer*, *Marketing agency*. Use the medium description. |
| 2 | **Clutch** (clutch.co) | Agency directory often cited for "best … agency" questions (Q14, Q16, Q20). | Free basic profile. Clutch interviews clients for verified reviews; 2–3 clients is enough to start. | Service lines: *Graphic Design*, *Web Design*, *Branding*. Industry focus: *Energy & natural resources*. |
| 3 | **PDAC corporate membership** | The industry's main body; members are listed and seen by every junior. | Paid annual membership (fee depends on category). | Category: *Non-mining: service/equipment supplier to the mineral industry*. |
| 4 | **AME (Association for Mineral Exploration)** | BC's exploration association; company profiles live on amebc.ca. | Paid company membership. | Supplier / service company. Use the long description. |
| 5 | **MSABC (Mining Suppliers Association of BC)** | The supplier association. (MABC itself represents producers, so it's a weaker fit.) | Paid membership. | Consultant / services. |
| 6 | **LinkedIn company page** | Read by ChatGPT, Copilot and Grok; also where your buyers are. | Free; you already have one. | Paste the long description into *About*, the tagline into *Tagline*, the services list into *Specialties*. |

**Suggested order:** 1 and 6 now (free, about 30 minutes in total), then 2 (free; needs a
few client reviews), then decide between 3, 4 and 5 by budget. PDAC gives the widest
reach. AME, plus Roundup in January, is the strongest BC network.

**Reviews to request first:** your three on-site testimonials (Snowline, West Red Lake,
Hercules) are the natural first asks for Google and Clutch reviews.
