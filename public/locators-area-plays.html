Investor Maps

Locators & area plays.

The first map an investor sees — the one that places your project on the world stage. Locators and area plays show where you sit relative to the majors, past producers, and the belts that define a district, so the opportunity reads in seconds.

From $1,200

Why it matters

Context is the first conviction.

Before an investor evaluates a single drill hole, they ask one question: where is this, and what’s around it? A great locator answers instantly — positioning your ground inside a proven mineral belt, beside the deposits and producers the market already trusts.

We build these as designed cartographic systems: accurate terrain, clean hierarchy, restrained colour, and the right neighbours labelled — the kind of figure that anchors a news release, a deck cover, or a website hero.

Selected Work

Locators & area plays we’ve made.

NGEX Resources

Vicuña District, Chile & Argentina

A regional locator placing NGEX’s Los Helados inside the Vicuña District — flanked by the Maricunga and El Indio porphyry belts and the names the market knows: Caserones, Cerro Casale, and the BHP / Lundin Vicuña project. Borders, infrastructure, and scale all read at a glance.

Kincora Copper

Macquarie Arc, New South Wales

An area play mapping Kincora’s full project portfolio across the Macquarie Arc and Cobar Basin systems, colour-coded by metal endowment and set against district anchors — Cadia, Northparkes, Cowal — plus partnered ground. It turns a scattered land package into a single, legible story.

G Mining Ventures

Guiana Shield & West Africa

A continental-scale locator placing G Mining’s Oko West, Gurupi, and Tocantinzinho among the Birimian / Transamazonian greenstone belts — reunited across the Pangea reconstruction to show the shared geology that hosts West Africa’s and South America’s largest gold deposits.
