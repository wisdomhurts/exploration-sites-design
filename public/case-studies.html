Case Studies

The geology never changed.
The comprehension did.

Years of investor-grade communication. The pattern we see: when the science is legible, investors can finally weigh what the geology is worth.

Read the case study →
TSX:MAU
Client since 2023

Koné Gold in Côte d’Ivoire was a strong West African gold system most investors couldn’t place on a map. We produced the investor maps that carried every resource update — from first inferred ounces through each expansion — and the same visual language ran through their corporate presentations, news releases, and roadshow decks.

Over this period

As the resource grew, the visuals scaled with it — institutional investors could read scale, grade, and continuity at a glance. Over the same period the company’s reported market capitalisation grew from ~$130M to ~$6.9B. The maps made the geology legible; the drilling, resource growth, and financings drove the valuation (company-reported, shown for context — not a claim of attribution).

What we produced
  • Client since 2023
  • Investor maps for every resource update
  • Corporate presentations
  • News-release graphics
  • Roadshow decks

Company-reported market cap ~$130M (2023) to ~$6.9B (today) — company disclosure; shown for context, not a claim of attribution.

TSXV:FWZ
Client since 2018

In 2018 we produced a GIS movie about Fireweed's deposits. Since then we've created their investor maps and run their website — work that ran alongside their growth from a micro-cap zinc explorer to a billion-dollar critical minerals company with the world's largest tungsten deposit. Their discoveries and financings built that value; we kept it legible to the market.

Over this period

Over seven years the company grew from a $16.5M micro-cap to a billion-dollar critical-minerals company and secured $35.4M in joint US–Canadian government funding (company-reported). We produced the investor communication throughout.

What we produced
  • Client since 2018
  • GIS deposit movie (2018)
  • Investor maps
  • Website (ongoing)

Company-reported market cap ~$16.5M (2018) to ~$1.03B (today) — company disclosure; shown for context, not a claim of attribution.

The Takeaway

Same rocks. Same management. A clearer story.

We don't take credit for the drill results. We don't move the rocks. But when the geology is ready to be seen — when the assays come back strong and the resource is growing — the companies that win are the ones whose story is already legible.

That's what we build. The maps, the websites, the presentations, the brand, the investor communications — the entire visual layer that turns invisible geology into something an allocator can evaluate. We've done it 500+ times across five continents. And the pattern repeats: when the understanding changes, companies are better positioned to compete for capital.
