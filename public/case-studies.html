Step 01 / 04
01 — The deposit

Koné Gold, Côte d’Ivoire.

A West African gold system that most investors wouldn’t find on a map. The geology was strong. The story wasn’t legible.

02 — The work

Locators, property maps, cross-sections.

We produced the investor maps that carried every resource update — from first inferred ounces through each expansion. The same visual language appeared in corporate presentations, news releases, and roadshow decks.

03 — The growth

Every stage, consistently told.

As the resource grew, the visuals scaled with it. Institutional investors could read the story at a glance because the visual system had been engineered to communicate scale, grade, and continuity without translation.

04 — The result

$67M to $6.0B. 90x.

Same deposit. Same management. The story became investable. This is what happens when geology is translated into the language capital markets already speak.

The Takeaway

The geology never changed. The comprehension did.

We don't take credit for the drill results. We don't move the rocks. But when the geology is ready to be seen — when the assays come back strong and the resource is growing — the companies that win are the ones whose story is already investable.

That's what we build. The maps, the websites, the presentations, the brand, the investor communications — the entire visual layer that turns invisible geology into capital markets conviction. We've done it 500+ times across five continents. And the pattern is always the same: the understanding changes, and the capital follows.
