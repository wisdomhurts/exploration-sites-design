Engagement

How we engage.

Three ways to work together — discrete project work, a complete launch package, and ongoing retainers — each scoped to your project, your timeline, and the standard the capital markets expect.

Model 01 À la carte

Project work.

From $1,200

Pick exactly the deliverables you need — investor maps, decks, websites, fact sheets, branding, or GIS films. Each piece is scoped on its own terms and delivered against a single fixed quote, so you know what you’re committing to before we begin.

Ideal for Companies addressing a specific gap — a deck ahead of a roadshow, a refreshed map set for a financing, a fact sheet before a conference — without committing to a broader program.

From $24,000

Monthly retainer.

From $1,900/mo

For companies in continuous market — ongoing map updates, presentation refreshes, site maintenance, and investor support, retained at a predictable monthly commitment.

[ §04 / 04 ]  Method

How an engagement works.

No open-ended estimates. No surprises. Approval before any work begins.

  1. 01/05

    Discovery call

    We learn about your deposit, your raise, and your timeline — and where the gaps are in how the market sees you.

  2. 02/05

    Scope & quote

    We define the deliverables and return a clear, fixed quote — no open-ended estimates, no surprises.

  3. 03/05

    You approve

    You review the scope and quote, ask questions, and give us the go-ahead before any work begins.

  4. 04/05

    We build

    We produce the work to an institutional standard, with checkpoints so you stay informed throughout.

  5. 05/05

    Ongoing support

    As your story advances, we keep your materials current — on a project basis or under a retainer.
