Investor Maps

Geology, geochem & geophysics.

Geological, geochemical, and geophysical maps — soil grids, magnetics, IP chargeability, and more — that show why the targets sit where they do.

Why it matters

The evidence behind the targets.

A locator says where; geoscience maps say why here. Lithology and structure, soil and rock geochemistry, magnetics, gravity, and IP chargeability are the layers that let an investor trace a target back to real, surveyed evidence — the difference between a story and a thesis.

We build each one as a designed figure: defensible interpolation, the right colour ramp for the data, clean contours, and a legend a generalist can read. Often we stack several datasets — geology under geochem under geophysics — so the coincident anomaly that ranks the target reads in a single glance.

Selected Work

A few we’ve made.

Property geology

Lithology & structure

A property-scale geology map — mapped units, contacts, and the structural framework that controls mineralisation — with custom symbology and a legend built for a non-geologist. It is the base layer every other dataset is read against.

Geochemistry

Soil & rock geochem

Sample-grid results rendered as interpolated surfaces with the anomalies called out — the footprint of a system at surface. Done well, the contour does the arguing: the high-tenor zone is obvious, and the drill plan that follows it makes sense on sight.

Geophysics

Magnetics, gravity & IP

Airborne and ground survey data — magnetics, gravity, IP chargeability — rendered with professional colour ramps and the interpreted structures drawn on. The geophysical signature that defines the buried target, made readable for the market.
