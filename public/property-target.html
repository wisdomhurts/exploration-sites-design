Investor Maps

Property & target maps.

Property, drill-plan, and target maps that orient investors on the ground — claims, infrastructure, drilling, and the priority targets that drive the program.

Why it matters

The map of what happens next.

Once an investor accepts the district and the geology, the next question is practical: what does the company actually hold, and what is it going to drill? Property and target maps answer it — claim boundaries, access and infrastructure, drilling completed and planned, and the ranked targets the program is built around.

We draw these from your tenure files, survey data, and drill database so they stay accurate as the program moves. Done right, a target map carries the whole exploration thesis on one page: here is the ground, here is what we’ve found, here is where the next holes go and why.

Selected Work

A few we’ve made.

Property & claims

The ground you hold

Claim boundaries, ownership, access roads, and infrastructure on a clean topographic base. The map that tells an investor exactly what the company controls and how easily it can be worked — tenure as an asset, drawn to scale.

Drill plan

What’s been drilled

Collar locations, hole traces, and significant intercepts laid over geology — the record of work done and the spacing that frames what comes next. It turns a press-release table of holes into a picture an investor can actually hold in their head.

Targets

Where the next holes go

Priority targets ranked and located against the data that generated them — geochem, geophysics, structure, and prior drilling. The forward-looking figure that shows the program has running room and a reason for every drill pad.
